# Invox

Smart email classification that organizes your inbox automatically.

Invox reads your incoming emails and intelligently sorts them into meaningful categories — so you never have to manually triage your inbox again. It separates spam from real messages, then routes each email to the right place: work, finance, travel, purchases, personal, and more.

No rules to write. No folders to set up. Just drop in an email and get back a clean classification — fast, accurate, and entirely automated.

## What It Does

- **Filters junk automatically** — spam and phishing never reach your attention
- **Categorizes every email** — from meetings and invoices to travel bookings and promotions
- **Understands nuance** — distinguishes a work project update from a project invoice, a personal email from a social invitation
- **Works out of the box** — no training data, no manual configuration, no ongoing maintenance

## Why Invox

| Instead of this | Invox does this |
|---|---|
| Manually reading and sorting every email | Automatically classifies in seconds |
| Writing complex filter rules that break | No rules needed — AI understands content |
| Missing important messages in noise | Spam is filtered before you see it |
| One-size-fits-all sorting | Rich categories that actually match your life |

## What It Covers

Invox recognizes a broad spectrum of email types — from professional correspondence and financial documents to travel itineraries, educational notices, security alerts, social messages, promotions, and personal matters. Each email gets sorted into a general category and a specific subcategory, giving you the right level of detail at a glance.

## Getting Started

Invox runs wherever you need it — pass an email and get a clean classification back in seconds. Integrate it into your workflow with a simple API call or command-line invocation.
